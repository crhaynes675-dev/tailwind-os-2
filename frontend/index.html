💡 Subcontractor Tips
Margin vs. Markup
A 25% margin ≠ 25% markup. 25% margin = 33.3% markup on cost.
Target Range
Aim for 25–35% gross margin to cover overhead, insurance, and profit.
Hidden Costs
Always factor in callbacks, warranty work, and unbillable drive time.
Break-Even
Know your monthly overhead so you can price above your break-even rate.

🔨 Section 3 — In‑House Job Pricing
What are Man‑Hours?
A man‑hour is the work done by one person in one hour. If a 3‑person crew works for 8 hours, that's 24 man‑hours — even though the job only took 1 day. Use man‑hours (not crew hours) when pricing in‑house labor so your rate accurately reflects the total human effort invested in a job.
